Substitute Harry

Privacy Policy
AI-powered Discord application

1. Introduction

Substitute Harry is an AI-powered Discord application created for the community surrounding YouTuber Mini Harry.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use Substitute Harry and how that information is handled.

2. Information We Process

When you interact with Substitute Harry, the application may process information required to provide its features, including:

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord username and display name
  • Server and channel information
  • Messages or commands sent to the bot
  • Information necessary to generate AI responses

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by Substitute Harry may be used to:

  • Respond to commands and messages
  • Generate AI-powered responses
  • Operate and maintain the Discord application
  • Prevent abuse and troubleshoot technical problems

4. AI Processing

Substitute Harry uses artificial intelligence to generate responses. Messages sent to the bot may be processed by third-party AI services when necessary to provide the AI functionality.

5. Data Storage

Substitute Harry does not intentionally permanently store conversations unless storage is required for a specific feature or explicitly disclosed to users.

Temporary processing may occur while generating an AI response.

6. Data Sharing

We do not intentionally sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the application, such as AI or hosting providers.

7. Data Deletion

If you believe Substitute Harry has stored information associated with you and you would like it removed, contact the application developer through the relevant Discord server.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Substitute Harry's features or data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or Substitute Harry, please contact the application developer through the Discord community where the application is provided.
