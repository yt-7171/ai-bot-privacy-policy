AI Bot Privacy Policy

Information Used

This Discord application may process Discord user IDs, usernames, server IDs, channel information, and messages sent to the bot. This information is used to provide the bot's features and generate AI responses.

Data Storage

The bot does not intentionally permanently store users' conversations unless explicitly stated by the developer.

AI Processing

Messages may be processed by third-party AI services required to generate responses.

Data Deletion

If information is stored by the application, users may request its deletion by contacting the developer.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the application developer through the Discord server associated with the application.
