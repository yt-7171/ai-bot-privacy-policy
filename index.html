Privacy Policy

Overview

Mini Harry AI is an AI-powered Discord application created for the Mini Harry community. This Privacy Policy explains what information the application may process and how it is used.

Information We Process

When you interact with Mini Harry AI, the application may process information such as your Discord user ID, username, server ID, channel information, commands, and messages sent to the bot.

This information is processed to provide the bot's AI responses, commands, moderation features, and other functionality.

AI Processing

Messages sent to Mini Harry AI may be processed by third-party AI services when necessary to generate an AI response.

Data Storage

Mini Harry AI does not intentionally permanently store conversations unless a feature specifically requires data to be stored.

Data Deletion

If information is stored by the application, users may request deletion of applicable stored information by contacting the bot's developer.

Third-Party Services

Mini Harry AI may use third-party services necessary to operate its AI functionality. Information sent to these services may be handled according to their respective privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Mini Harry AI's features or data practices change.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the developer through the official Mini Harry Discord community.
